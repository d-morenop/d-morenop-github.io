About me

I combine physical descriptions, numerical methods and stochastic processes to understand the stability of Thwaites under a warming climate. My research focuses on future behaviour of the Antarctic Ice Sheet.

My interests include ice sheet modelling, grounding line stability, and sea-level projections.
